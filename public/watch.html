What trips an alarm

  • A ghost appears — someone signed a new anonymous supply deal. New brain on the free shelf.
  • A ghost vanishes — a supply deal died or went paid. Watch which ghosts survive the cull.
  • The registry reshuffles — models.dev is opencode's public catalog for every tool; silent upstream swaps land here first.
  • A release ships — version heartbeats show the desk is alive and sometimes admit model changes in plain text.

Masks come off · twice in 12 months

Case 01 · April–May 2024 gpt2-chatbot → GPT-4o Mystery model topped the public arena under a joke name. A leaked error message exposed OpenAI's own infrastructure. Days later OpenAI confirmed it: GPT-4o in disguise, pre-launch. Ars Technica ↗ Case 02 · April 2025 Quasar / Optimus Alpha → GPT-4.1 Two cloaked free models de-gloved in days by pure forensics: OpenAI-format IDs in responses, the exact known tokenizer bug on Chinese characters, matching API quirks. Router confirmed both. OpenRouter blog ↗

Anonymity held about ten days in both cases. Raw access leaks metadata; tokenizers are fingerprints; labs eventually want credit. Geoff's layers hide more surface (stripped reasoning, wallet gate) — harder to unmask, same physics.
